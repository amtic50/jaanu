How Jaanu works 📒

Swipe the cards sideways — today's card is first. Tap a card's boxes each day. Every small thing now earns points — the score is shown on the card, out of 200%.

⭐ How the score works

  • Core — 100 points: gutka at or under the day's target 40 · no alcohol 20 · walk 20 (a 5-min walk earns ~3) · less-oil food 20.
  • Bonus — up to 100 more: sleep 25 (7 hours = full) · water 25 (8 glasses = full) · nicotine gum 15 (3 per gum, max 5) · black coffee 15 · mood 5 · one-line note 5 · period day 10.
  • 100% = the day's challenge beaten. A ⭐ star still needs all 4 core tasks done — stars, streaks and levels work exactly as before. A perfect day (all 4 core tasks) still earns ⭐ 1 star.

🎮 Levels

  • Lv 1 Seed · Lv 2 Sprout (7-day streak) · Lv 3 Bloom (14) · Lv 4 Glow (21) · Lv 5 Star (28) · Lv 6+ Superstar
  • Streak milestones (7, 14, 21, 30, 45, 60 days) get a celebration 🎉
