How Jaanu works 📒

Swipe the cards sideways — today's card is first. Tap a card's boxes each day. 4 key boxes make a perfect day: gutka at or under the day's target, no alcohol, 30-minute walk, less-oil food. A perfect day earns ⭐ 1 star. 7 perfect days in a row = a new level.

🎮 Levels

  • Lv 1 Seed · Lv 2 Sprout (7-day streak) · Lv 3 Bloom (14) · Lv 4 Glow (21) · Lv 5 Star (28) · Lv 6+ Superstar
  • Streak milestones (7, 14, 21, 30, 45, 60 days) get a celebration 🎉

🌸 Trying (Month 3)

Log period days honestly. Jaanu estimates the fertile window from your logged period start (about cycle days 10–17) and marks those cards 🌸 — but only in Month 3, and only if gutka and alcohol are at zero and the checks (TSH result) are in. Otherwise Month 3 becomes a correction month and trying shifts one cycle. Do not restart the thyroid tablet on your own.
